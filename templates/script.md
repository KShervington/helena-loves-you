Rules (from 04):
- Hook is a complete thought in ≤ 12 words, spoken AND on screen (different wording is fine).
- Open the gap in beat 1–2; re-open it in beat 5 ("but here's the catch…").
- One new idea per beat. If a beat needs two diagrams, split the beat.
- The analogy appears in beat 3 and is paid off or broken in beat 5/6.
- CTA: "Comment the next thing you want explained." + a quiet "follow for the next one".
- On-screen text ≤ 8 words per line, ≤ 2 lines; it stays up ≥ 0.3 s per word.
